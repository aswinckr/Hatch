## Workflow

You will have a codebase that you can access, which contains a screen, and you will have a link to the design that you can access via MCP on Penpot.

Your task is to compare what was implemented against the design step by step. Look at each component, and then turn it into an HTML file highlighting all the differences.

I will also provide a visual example of how to design the HTML page where you can show the before and after and mark different parts of the UI that require changes.
