# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

À la Card is for people who take photos of food at restaurants, at friends’ homes, and in their own kitchens, then lose those photos in a crowded camera roll. When deciding what to cook, they want to rediscover those dishes as useful inspiration instead of repeatedly searching through old photos.

## Product Purpose

The current product is a marketing site for the À la Card concept. It explains how someone could turn the food photos already on their phone into an ongoing personal menu of recipes. Success for the site means visitors quickly understand that their existing photo library can become an organized source of cooking inspiration.

## Positioning

À la Card begins with an overlooked source that conventional recipe libraries do not: the user’s existing food-photo archive. It turns those buried memories into a browsable personal menu, associating the dishes in the photos with recipes the user can return to when deciding what to cook.

## Operating Context

Visitors encounter À la Card through a self-contained marketing page. The proposed product workflow begins with food photos already stored on a phone, including dishes photographed at restaurants, at friends’ homes, or at home. Those photos become entries in an ongoing personal recipe menu that the user can browse later for cooking inspiration. This menu is a living recipe library, not a printable menu for a single meal.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current deliverable is only a static marketing site.
- The site explains the proposed photo-library-to-recipe-menu workflow but does not implement photo access, dish recognition, recipe creation, a recipe gallery, search, editing, accounts, analytics, persistence, or backend services.
- The page is a self-contained HTML document with embedded CSS and same-page navigation. It works without a build step or external dependency.
- Future marketing copy must describe an ongoing personal menu built from the user’s existing food photos, not a printable restaurant-style menu assembled for one dinner.
- The detailed behavior for recognizing dishes, deriving or sourcing recipes, organizing the library, search, and photo privacy remains undecided.

## Brand Commitments

The confirmed product name is “À la Card.” The product should communicate its purpose in approachable language for people who want cooking inspiration from food memories already stored on their phones.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains a single static landing page at `index.html`. Its current copy emphasizes printable restaurant-style menu cards and does not yet represent the confirmed personal recipe-library concept. There is no functional product, customer evidence, testimonials, pricing, benchmark data, or live demonstration. Future work must not fabricate those forms of proof.

## Product Principles

1. Make the buried-food-photos problem immediately recognizable.
2. Turn existing memories into useful cooking inspiration with minimal effort.
3. Treat the personal menu as a living recipe library that becomes more useful over time.
4. Distinguish clearly between the proposed product and functionality actually available on the marketing site.
