# Design QA report

## Run conditions

Reference, app URL, date, viewport and scale, starting state, included scope, time budget, and evidence limitations.

## Coverage

| Region or check | Status | Evidence or limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |

Statuses: matched, different, not applicable, unverified.

## Findings

For each supported finding:

- **Location:** Section, component, and repeated instances checked.
- **Type:** Visual difference / reproduced interaction issue / usability hypothesis.
- **Reference:** Observed expected treatment or supplied requirement.
- **App:** Observed treatment or reproduced behavior.
- **Evidence:** Paired image names or links and the relevant region; reproduction steps for behavior.
- **Correction:** A concrete adjustment supported by the reference.
- **Impact:** Practical consequence, with inferred consequences labeled.
- **Confidence and uncertainty:** Explain any measurement, scaling, state, or coverage limitation.

## Remaining work

Unverified regions, unavailable states, and questions requiring additional evidence. Do not declare the whole screen correct from partial coverage.
