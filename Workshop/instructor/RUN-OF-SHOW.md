## Engagement cues

- Before a demo, ask for a prediction. During waiting time, pairs make the same comparison manually.
- Switch roles after each run: one person drives; the other challenges one finding against the evidence.
- End every activity with a saved artifact and one sentence about the decision it changed.
- If setup takes longer, use the supplied skill or offline screenshot. Reduce sharing time before removing hands-on time.
- No extra explanation slides are needed: hold the activity slide while people work. Exact prompts and fallback actions are in the teacher guide and warmup handout.
